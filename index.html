The catalogue

Seven works. Three on the wall, four in the studio.

Numbered in learning order. Pointer Chain follows the graph chapter because BFS and DFS lean on its queue and stack, and the heap comes next because Dijkstra’s priority queue is one.

    How it works

    Every chapter plays like a film you can hold.

    The algorithm runs first and records each step. The sculpture then performs those steps, so you can pause on any of them, go back, or slow the whole thing to a quarter of its speed.

    Every step is recorded

    The algorithm runs instantly and keeps a snapshot of each step. Play, pause, step, rewind, or slow it to a quarter speed.

    It reads along with you

    A plain-English caption for every step says what happened and why, down to the comparison that sent a value left or the ticket that moved up the queue.

    Poke at anything

    Orbit and zoom. Tap a node to see its numbers. Build your own inputs, race two algorithms, and break things on purpose.
